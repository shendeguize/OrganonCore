# Organon Core Absorb

Read the adopted philosophy using [philosophy resolution](../references/philosophy-resolution.md) and the [shared iteration, maintenance, dependency, and authority rules](../references/iteration.md). Communicate in the user's language. This skill coordinates philosophical absorption; absorption is not a universal obligation inferred from self-transcendence.

## Establish the question and current relationship

Identify the input, selected current philosophical baseline, alleged gap, intended contribution, and any intended adoption target. An advisory review may continue without a selected target; resolving a baseline does not select a write destination. Read the relevant existing material before assuming a gap. Establish the input's grounds, conditions, and support limits. Pass the selected baseline to assessment and independent reviewers; shared Core references do not change the target to Core. Use external grilling when explicitly requested or needed for material unknowns and trade-offs, after gathering discoverable facts.

Before adoption, establish the exact target and read and preserve any existing adopted text; record absence when the target is new. If the existing text differs from the selected baseline, distinguish the target's own commitments from the external assessment basis and assess the proposed revision against both, identifying the standard for each judgment. Do not replace either text with the other. When a target is established or changes, supplement the affected judgments and review; keep the current assessment baseline fixed unless a separate assessment explicitly selects another baseline. Target selection does not supply adoption authorization.

Arrange independent review under the shared protocol: the reviewer receives the raw input, baseline, objectives, and necessary context before the author’s candidate or conclusions. It records its initial judgment before those are disclosed. The author can develop the proposal independently while that review proceeds.

Use [organon-core-assess](../organon-core-assess/SKILL.md) to establish existing coverage, compatibility, conflicts, and support limits, reusing a relevant assessment when the baseline and evidence remain applicable. Keep this judgment separate from whether revision is warranted. Compatibility alone does not justify adoption; conflict with a current commitment does not by itself refute an argument for changing it. Name the commitment challenged and examine the reasons without silently substituting the candidate as the baseline.

## Develop the candidate and review its reasons

Compare retaining the current form with meaningful alternatives: adding, clarifying, revising, merging, or withdrawing philosophical content. Explain what understanding or constructive capability would be gained, or what unsupported or conflicting content would be corrected. Novelty, volume, or a useful but already covered example need not warrant a change. Give reasons for no absorption or deferral when appropriate; a covered input with no full-review trigger can end with that bounded conclusion.

Specify concrete provisions, their meanings and application limits, the grounds for changing them, additional premises, alternatives, and consequences. Identify which existing commitments would remain or be withdrawn. A hypothetical revised whole is a candidate, not the adopted baseline; old and new commitments need not remain simultaneously held after an explicit withdrawal. A downstream adopter may revise or withdraw a Core commitment; distinguish the selected philosophy's judgments from the Core method's constraints, and report any method limitation separately rather than silently vetoing the candidate because it differs from Core.

Use [organon-core-principled-review](../organon-core-principled-review/SKILL.md) when its full-review triggers apply. Review the substantive candidate, the reasons for adoption, the relationships within the proposed whole, and this absorption method and its criteria. Reuse findings with their standards and support limits; supplement them for material changes instead of duplicating unchanged checks. A method preference is not automatically a core obligation, and completing the review does not establish that adoption is warranted.

Distinguish philosophical change from necessary synchronization. A change to a commitment, meaning, or applicability belongs to the selected philosophy and requires the user's explicit decision. Update that adoption target's rationale, translations, or skills where the adopted change requires it; reasons and examples do not independently add philosophical obligations. A downstream adoption does not authorize changes or publication to Core. An explanatory edit that changes a required judgment is a possible philosophical change. Independent skill optimization or general documentation work belongs to shared maintenance, even if the absorption discussion revealed it; do not treat discovering useful work as authorization to perform it.

## Resolve feedback, decide, and implement

Present the candidate and its consequences, gather material feedback, and revise or retain unresolved questions with reasons. Define acceptance situations and counterexamples before implementation. Apply the shared maintenance checks to the proposal and its necessary synchronization, including requirements, authorization, actual validation evidence, wording, and translation correspondence. Method changes retain the repository’s requirements to explain their formation or revision and assess a relevant variation.

After the independent initial judgment is recorded, provide the candidate and grounds for cross-review. The reviewer must address both the relationship to the original philosophy, using assess, and whether the reasons for revision are supported, using principled review when applicable. An assess result alone does not establish the case for adoption. Address material objections or preserve reasoned disagreement; delegated reviewers return findings without opening another review chain.

Default to a concrete, reviewable proposal. After the required review and user decision, execute changes covered by explicit authorization; do not ask again for the same authorized scope. New or materially changed philosophical meaning needs an explicit decision covering it. Keep the current baseline until the revision is actually adopted. Verify the resulting text and required synchronization against the reviewed, authorized change; rerun affected cases when material edits or failures warrant it. Producing a method text alone establishes neither improvement nor capability beyond what that evidence supports.

Record no absorption, deferral, proposed change, or completed adoption with its grounds, authorization, actual changes, and remaining limits. Use the shared bounded self-assessment and stopping rules. Neither agreement between agents nor more iterations proves correctness. Consult [validation cases](../references/validation.md) for development and comparison after independent test responses have been recorded.
